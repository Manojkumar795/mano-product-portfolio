Aircraft 360-degree preview: 720 local renders of the existing FlightGear-derived exterior at half-degree increments. Exactly one opaque angle is drawn per update. No additional runtime 3D model; the complete WebP atlas is fetched only while ALTITUDE is visible. Reproducible render script: scripts/assets/render-aircraft-sprites.py in the portfolio source.

Fixed demonstration itinerary: 8 October 2026, 11:40 PDT (18:40 UTC), 12-hour journey, arriving 9 October 2026 at 15:40 JST (06:40 UTC). Times use America/Los_Angeles and Asia/Tokyo time zones. Remaining time is calculated from the same progress state as the globe. No live airline data.

Final refinement: bottom scrubber and reset controls removed; slide labels restart animations without remounting scenes. The geographic camera turns faster initially, easing into a slow orbit independent of demo playback. The itinerary, elapsed time, remaining time, phase and aircraft route position share one progress state.
