# Homiq product walkthrough and evidence panels

The hero presents five unchanged original app screens in a timed, automatically playing sequence. Layered graphite phone frames, perspective, soft shadows and restrained movement provide depth on the white stage. Visitors can pause or choose a screen. Playback stops outside the viewport, timers and observers clean up, and reduced-motion preferences prevent automatic playback. The presentation is identified as a walkthrough of original screens, rather than a live working application or recorded video.

Research now pairs three source-linked design priorities with original room-list and lamp screens. The screen-audit summary uses a single consistent surface with concrete labels for coverage, the three main tabs and named confirmations. Counts and evidence qualifications remain unchanged.

The previous uneven scene/state cards and oversized trailing gap are replaced by two equal-width, equal-height cards in a shared grid. Both use 126px original screens and aligned captions. They stack on small displays. The surrounding existing case-study sections and other portfolio projects are retained.

Verification: production build and artifact validation passed, as did all 19 existing tests. All 27 sections were checked at 1920, 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 390 and 375px. No page overflow, section overlaps or text overflow were detected. The paired cards match dimensions at every width; all hero frames load and playback controls remain within the hero. Native-browser checks verified autoplay progression, pause, manual frame selection, playback resume and offscreen pause. All supplied PNGs remain byte-identical.
